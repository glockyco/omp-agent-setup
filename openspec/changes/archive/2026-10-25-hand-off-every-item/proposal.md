# Proposal

## Why

The `notes` vault now treats papers, projects, supervisions, talks and topics alike as items, and each item note starts with a `Resume here:` line. The handoff rule still covers only projects, papers and supervisions, so a session that prepares the slides of a talk or works on a research topic leaves its handoff stale.

## What Changes

- The project handoff rule covers every item of the vault: papers, projects, supervisions, talks and topics.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `personal-omp-plugin`: the project handoff rule.

## Impact

`plugin/rules/project-handoff.md`.
