## Authority

- Standard: ASD-STE100, Simplified Technical English.
- Issue: Issue 9, January 2025.
- Owner: ASD Simplified Technical English Maintenance Group (STEMG).
- Official definition: <https://www.asd-ste100.org/about_STE.html>
- Official Issue 9 copy: <https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/ASD-STE100_ISSUE9.pdf>
- Official downloads page: <https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html>
- AI guidance: <https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/WhitePaper-ASD-STE100_and_AI.pdf>

ASD-STE100 is a controlled natural language for technical information. Issue 9 organizes 53 writing rules into nine sections. The rules work with a controlled dictionary and with approved technical nouns and technical verbs.

## Dictionary boundary

The official dictionary controls approved words, meanings, parts of speech, and forms. The complete dictionary is copyrighted and is not reproduced here. A strict compliance review requires the official standard and dictionary.

Code, identifiers, commands, paths, product names, quotations, and domain terms can be technical names. This allowance does not remove the rules for consistent meaning and grammar.
